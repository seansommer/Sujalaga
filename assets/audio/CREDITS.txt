SUJALAGA — arcade soundtrack and sound effects

All recordings below were released by their creators under Creative Commons
Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Retrieved October 19, 2026. Source recordings converted to MP3 for the game.
Music: stereo 44.1 kHz / 128 kbps. Effects and voices: mono 32 kHz / 80 kbps.
Voice clips trimmed and loudness-normalized to -17 LUFS.
No AI music service was used.
