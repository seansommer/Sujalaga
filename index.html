FLIGHT SCHOOL

FILL. CAP. FLY.

Your nozzles fire automatically. Slide your finger in the clear space below your ship to steer without covering it. Move side to side, or a little forward and backward. You can also drag on the play area, or use arrow keys / WASD. Lift and reposition your finger whenever you like.

01

Fill the fleet. Each hit raises the juice inside a bottle. Fill it completely and watch it cap and fly away. Diving bottles earn 50% extra points.

02

Keep your hull. Dodge enemy shots and diving bottles. Each boss brings its own weapon: plasma orbs, citrus discs, carrot rockets, or berry comets. You have three hull points; a boss clear restores one. Getting hit resets your combo.

03

Catch an upgrade. Fly into a glowing token: twin nozzles, rapid fill, wide spray, or a bubble shield. Nozzle upgrades last 16 seconds.

04

Unleash Juice Surge. Filling bottles charges your surge. The glass meter on the right fills upward. Tap its flashing ϟ or press Space when it is ready to clear incoming fire and fill a whole group at once.

Arcade: clear all 16 waves across four juice sectors. Endless: the fleet keeps coming. Practice: unlimited hull, no competitive score. Fast wave clears earn a bonus. Clean streaks build up to ×4 points.
